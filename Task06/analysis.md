# Task 6 – College Canteen Inventory and Billing

Entities: Item, Date, Clock, Sale/Bill. Item is a tagged union: packaged item carries expiry date; cooked item carries preparation time; combo carries up to three component IDs. Inventory is an array of Item structures. Sale pseudocode: locate item; reject invalid quantity or quantity greater than stock; decrement stock; add quantity×price to revenue; record bill line. Expiry pseudocode: scan packaged items and compare expiry date to today's date plus two days. Revenue summary sorts by revenue descending. The demonstration uses sample dates anchored to the assignment's sample execution; replace with current-date logic for production. A linked list allows O(1) insertion/removal when node is known, O(n) search, and incurs one next pointer per node; array search is O(n) and removal may shift elements.
